# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want an open, welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, expression, background, appearance, ability, religion, or technical opinion.

## Expected behavior

- Show empathy, respect, and curiosity.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Provide context, avoid exclusionary language, and help people who are learning.
- Respect privacy and personal boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized content or unwanted sexual attention.
- Sharing private information without consent.
- Trolling, stalking, or deliberately disrupting discussions.
- Any conduct a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions and restrict participation by anyone who violates this code. Decisions should consider context, impact, recurrence, and the safety of the people involved.

Report incidents privately to a maintainer through the contact channels published on the repository profile. Do not expose the affected person in a public issue. Reports will be handled confidentially and reviewed in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies within project spaces and whenever someone publicly represents Flowboard.
